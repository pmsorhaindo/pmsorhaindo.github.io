repo-radar_

A read-only status snapshot of Michael's side projects on GitHub. One command tells you which projects are moving, which are rotting, and which have quietly fallen 877 commits behind.

Why it exists

"What's the status of my Turing machine mod?" shouldn't take ten minutes of clicking through PR tabs. A builder bot should answer that in one breath, with facts, not vibes. And it should say stalled before you notice it yourself.

What it does

Finds the live ones
Own repos and forks pushed in the last 90 days, plus anything with open PRs or issues.
Fork drift
How far each fork is ahead of or behind its upstream, via GitHub's compare API.
PR forensics
Draft or not, age, idle days, head SHA, CI verdict, how far behind its base it is, and stacked-PR detection.
Stall alarm
Flags a PR idle for 7 days or more, failing checks, an outdated base, or a fork behind upstream. Dependabot spam gets folded away into a "Dormant" pile.

How it works

  • One Python file, standard library only, plus the authenticated gh CLI. GET requests only, so it can't break anything.
  • A 5-minute on-disk cache: about 35s cold, about 1s warm. It waits out short rate limits and retries server hiccups with backoff.
  • Commit statuses and check runs are merged into one verdict. No CI shows as "none", not as a green tick, because it hasn't earned one.
  • Every timestamp is converted from UTC to Amsterdam time and labelled CEST or CET.
  • Output as --md for humans, --json for bots, --repo owner/name for a deep dive, and --public-only for pages like this one.
